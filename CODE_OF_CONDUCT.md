# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small project and there's no need for anything
more elaborate than that.

## What's expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about tradeoffs, not character.
- Critique code, not people.
- Accept that maintainers will sometimes say no, and that "no" isn't hostility.
- Respect that contributors have different levels of experience, and that
  someone asking a basic question is doing the right thing by asking.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Demeaning comments about anyone's identity — including race, gender, gender
  identity, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, age, nationality, or
  religion.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commits, and anywhere else someone is
representing the project.

## Reporting

Email **conduct@cognify.software**. Reports go only to the maintainers, and
we'll keep the reporter's identity private.

We'll acknowledge within 3 working days. Depending on what happened, the
response may be a private word, a request for a public apology, temporary
suspension from the repo, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who break these rules
face the same consequences as anyone else.

If your report is about a maintainer and you'd rather not email that address,
say so in a GitHub private vulnerability report and we'll route it.
